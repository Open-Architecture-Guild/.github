# Open Architecture Guild 🌍
*A collaborative, open-source initiative defining global standards in enterprise architecture*

---

## Welcome
Enterprise Architecture (EA) and business design have traditionally been locked behind proprietary vendor walls, costly consulting frameworks, or reinvented from scratch by every organization. 

We believe there is a better way. This organization is home to resources designed to bridge the gap between high-level business strategy and technical execution. By pooling collective architectural intelligence, we create living, universally understood standards that accelerate digital transformation.

---

## Key Repositories
* **[`capability-map`](https://github.com/your-org/capability-map)** *(Example)*: The core repository containing the open-source business capability map, structured hierarchically in clean YAML and Markdown formats.

---

## Core Principles
1. **Business-Centric**: Focuses purely on *what* an organization does, entirely independent of *how*, *where*, or *by whom* it is executed.
2. **Stable & Outcome-Oriented**: Built around long-term enterprise capabilities tied directly to business outcomes and KPIs.
3. **Open & Transparent**: Peer-reviewed by a global community of architecture professionals.
